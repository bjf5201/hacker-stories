# CurioSearch :mag_right:

Small web app that let's like-minded people *discover and share interesting blog posts* in a visually engaging way, without robbing their attention spans. User's can search a curated library of posts, add their own finds, and filter results by keywords or category tags.

This project started out as I was following [Robin Wieruch's](https://robinwieruch.de) wonderful book, [Road to React](https://roadtoreact.com). I already knew React, but I had learned it "on the job", meaning I always felt like I was putting out fires and never had the time to learn it piece by piece. This project was me giving myself that change, while also adding a few bells and whistles that tickled my fancy. :sparkles:

## Use Cases

**1. Personal Knowledge Management**
This app is not trying to compete with PKM giants such as Obsidian or Notion, but is instead following the Linux approach of "Do one thing and do it well". I, personally, use Obsidian and often stress about taking notes and sourcing my notes properly. I would use this app to keep all my sources collected in one place and be able to reference them easily from Obsidian (think Zotero, but for developers rather than PhD candidates)

**2. Team Resource Sharing**
A small remote team could use it as a private, lightweight way to share articles related to their project or industry without setting up heavier tools like Notion or Confluence

**3. Niche Community Newsletter Curation**
A hobbyist community (i.e., photographers, activists, indie devs, or tabletop gamers) could use it to maintain a public list of recommended articles, tutorials, and guides relevant to their interest.
